About Us

Built on Digital Expertise.
Delivered with Construction Mastery.

Teraju Ciptabina Resources is a Certified CIDB Registered Contractor with PKK, SPKK, STB & MOF from Ministry Of Finance established in 2018, specialising in Design & Build, Interior Design & Fit-Out, and Building Information Modelling (BIM) solutions and training.

The company builds on the proven foundation of Asyraf Reeza Studio & BIM Studio Sdn Bhd, a recognised BIM consultant and Archicad solution provider that previously partnered with Graphisoft and CIDBEC. This combination brings practical construction delivery capability and specialised digital BIM expertise under one roof.

Our new and larger office in Puchong is equipped with dedicated training tables and a spacious meeting room, enabling us to deliver hands-on Archicad BIM training programmes alongside construction and interior projects.
