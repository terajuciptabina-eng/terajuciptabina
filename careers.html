Careers at Teraju Ciptabina

We are always looking for motivated professionals who share our commitment to quality construction delivery and digital BIM excellence.

Why Work With Us

  • Hands-on experience in Design & Build and Interior Fit-Out projects across residential and commercial sectors.
  • Direct exposure to Archicad BIM workflows, training delivery, and Malaysian BIM requirements (including projects ≥ RM10 million).
  • Collaborative environment at our training-ready headquarters in Puchong with modern facilities.
  • Opportunity to grow with a CIDB G1, MOF-registered contractor that combines construction and digital expertise.

How to Apply

Send your CV and a brief cover letter indicating the role you are interested in to our email. We review applications on a rolling basis and will contact shortlisted candidates for an interview.
