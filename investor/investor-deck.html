03 · THE PROBLEM
Construction planning can be fragmented.
Scope & information

Project requirements can live across conversations, drawings, spreadsheets and documents.

Cost structure

Quantities, rates and area-level costs need consistent structure to support clearer decisions.

Disconnected workflows

Homeowner planning and contractor costing often happen in separate systems or documents.

Quotation as an endpoint

A quotation can become a static output instead of a structured record that supports the wider workflow.
