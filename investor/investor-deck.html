03 · THE PROBLEM
Construction decisions can become fragmented between cost, work, progress and payment.
Cost structure

Quantities, rates and scope can be difficult to maintain consistently when the starting point is only a lump-sum figure.

Project execution

Work programme, actual progress and payment can become separate records rather than one connected project reference.

Variations

Changes in scope and VO can become difficult to reconcile against the original budget.

Shared visibility

Homeowner and contractor may not have the same structured view of budget, work, progress and remaining value.

Problem03 / 16
04 · PROBLEM EVIDENCE
Two real construction scenarios illustrate the gap.
CASE 01 · CLUSTER HOUSE · TAMAN DESIRAN BAYU
RM250k lump-sum contract

Claims were made by percentage without clear item-level justification. The homeowner had paid more than 95% while physical progress was below 70%. VO and scope changes became difficult to reconcile and the project reached a funding gap before completion.

CASE 02 · BUNGALOW · PENANG
RM300k budget → RM420k spent

RM420k had been spent — RM120k above the original budget (+40%). Work remained unfinished, including touch-up / finishing items. The contractor could not continue as funds became insufficient, while the homeowner was unwilling to commit further payment.

Common failure point

Budget → Work → Progress → Claim → Payment → VO were not maintained as one shared structured record. These are problem scenarios, not claims about TERAJU WORKS customers.

11 · CONTRACTOR NETWORK
Make professional project control accessible to contractors of different sizes.
Vendor eligibility

Proposed baseline checks can include CIDB registration, applicable licences, company documentation and relevant credentials before a contractor joins the managed network.

TERAJU Professional Project Standard

Participating vendors can be expected to use structured costing, Work Programme, planned vs actual progress, S-Curve and project records to present a more systematic project workflow to homeowners.

Demand → Vendor

Homeowner projects captured through TERAJU WORKS can become future qualified project opportunities for eligible vendors. A project-based fee, such as 5%, is a commercial hypothesis to validate, not current revenue.

Future institutional layer

TERAJU may explore project administration, claim/payment workflows and longer-term alignment or integration opportunities with CIDB, JKR and relevant government bodies. No current partnership or endorsement is claimed.
