Cost transparency06 / 16
06 · COST TRANSPARENCY
When cost, progress and payment are disconnected, both sides can lose visibility.
CASE 01 · CLUSTER HOUSE · TAMAN DESIRAN BAYU
RM250k lump-sum contract

50% then 30% claims without clear item-level justification. Homeowner paid more than 95% while physical progress was below 70%. VO and scope changes became difficult to reconcile, and the project reached a funding gap before completion.

CASE 02 · BUNGALOW · PENANG
RM300k budget → RM420k spent

RM420k had already been spent — RM120k above the original budget (+40%). Work was still unfinished, including outstanding touch-up / finishing items. The contractor could not continue as funds became insufficient, while the homeowner was unwilling to commit further payment.

Common failure

Budget → Work → Progress → Claim → Payment → VO were not maintained as one shared structured record. TERAJU WORKS is designed to create that common reference, helping protect both homeowner and contractor.
