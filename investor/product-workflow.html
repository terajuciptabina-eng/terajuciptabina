TERAJU WORKS · PRODUCT EVIDENCE
From Project Input to Project Insight
The current workflow connects manual or AI-assisted room/area input with detailed construction costing and project control.
01 · INPUT
Manual or AI-assisted
Create rooms/areas manually, or upload a PDF/image floor plan.
02 · AI FLOOR PLAN
Automatic extraction
AI Vision extracts room names and explicitly stated areas from the drawing for review.
03 · BUILDPLANNER
Structured room & area
Reviewed extracted data can be imported into BuildPlanner and used as the costing input.
04 · COST
Detailed SMM estimate
Work items, quantities, rates and amounts form a structured cost baseline.
05 · CONTRACT
Confirmed baseline
The confirmed estimate carries contract value and project dates.
06 · EXECUTION
Work Program
Cost-estimate items flow into planned activities and timing.
07 · CONTROL
Planned vs Actual
Weekly actual progress can be compared against planned execution.
08 · INSIGHT
S-Curve & Project Insight
Progress, schedule and value are brought together into project-control insight.
Key product point: AI is an optional acceleration layer, not a required input method. The same structured room/area data can be created manually or extracted automatically from a PDF/image floor plan, then carried forward into the construction costing and project-control workflow.
