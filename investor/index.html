THE OPPORTUNITY

Construction decisions still depend heavily on fragmented information.

TERAJU WORKS is being developed around a practical problem: project information, quantities, rates, costing and quotation decisions can be difficult to structure consistently across a construction or renovation workflow.

⌁

Fragmented planning

Project information can start in conversations, drawings, spreadsheets and separate costing documents.

▤

Cost visibility

Homeowners and contractors need a clearer way to structure quantities, rates and area-by-area costs.

↗

Workflow continuity

The platform is being built to connect planning and costing workflows instead of treating each step as an isolated document.

THE PRODUCT TODAY

A working platform, not just a concept.

The current public product already has separate entry points for homeowners and contractors, with Build Planner and Renovation Planner flows inside TERAJU WORKS.

WHY THIS IS DIFFERENT

Built from construction experience, then translated into software.

TERAJU WORKS is being developed alongside an operating construction business, giving the product a direct connection to real renovation and building workflows.

The product direction combines practical construction knowledge, quantity/costing logic, design experience and software development rather than starting from a generic project-management template.

Development is founder-led and in-house. The product is under active development in a public GitHub repository, with the repository showing ongoing work through September 2026.

TARGET USERS

Two sides of the residential construction workflow.

Homeowners

People planning a new house or renovating an existing home who need clearer scope, quantities and budget visibility.

Contractors

Small and growing construction businesses that need a more structured digital workflow for estimating and quotations.
