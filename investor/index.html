INVESTOR CLARITY

The structure, stated plainly.

What exactly is the investment?

The intended investment target is TERAJUWORKS as a technology venture. It is not an investment into the general construction operations of TERAJU CIPTABINA RESOURCES.

Why is TERAJUWORKS currently under TERAJU CIPTABINA RESOURCES?

TERAJUWORKS is still at an early, founder-controlled stage. Keeping it under the existing business has allowed the founder to build and operate the product with minimal capital and administrative overhead.

What will investment change?

Capital is intended to support technology and commercial scale while also funding the appropriate IP, corporate, financial and governance structuring required to establish TERAJUWORKS as a dedicated technology venture.

Can TERAJU CIPTABINA RESOURCES use TERAJUWORKS?

Yes. It can be a customer and contractor within the ecosystem, including using TERAJUWORKS to produce estimates and manage its own projects. Its relationship with TERAJUWORKS should be documented as a commercial related-party relationship.

Can TERAJUWORKS scale without TERAJU CIPTABINA RESOURCES?

That is the intended direction. TERAJU CIPTABINA RESOURCES is an early operating environment and ecosystem participant; TERAJUWORKS is designed to serve independent homeowners and contractors beyond the founder's construction business.
