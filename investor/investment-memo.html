01 · EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

The investment proposition

TERAJU CIPTABINA is an early-stage company, not a scaled technology business. The company is building a technology layer around residential construction, starting with detailed, SMM-based cost estimation and extending into contract-linked project control and structured construction data.

TERAJU WORKS is the technology engine. It supports homeowner and contractor workflows, with manual or AI-assisted floor-plan input, detailed costing, contract setup, Work Programme, Actual vs Planned, S-Curve and Project Insight. Contractor rate observations and state-based market signals are being developed as a data layer.

The commercial model is not yet proven. There are no meaningful recurring software revenues and no paying TERAJU WORKS users at the current stage. The purpose of the proposed RM1M round is to move from founder-led product development into structured market validation, commercialisation and scalable execution.

03 · PROBLEM

When cost, work, progress, payment and variation are disconnected.

Construction projects can become difficult to reconcile when the original budget, detailed work items, progress claims, payments and variations are maintained in separate documents or informal records.

Cluster house · Taman Desiran Bayu

RM250k lump-sum contract; more than 95% paid while physical progress was below 70%; percentage claims and VO/scope changes became difficult to reconcile.

Bungalow · Penang

RM300k original budget; RM420k spent (+RM120k / +40%); project remained unfinished and the contractor could not continue as funds became insufficient.

Common reference

TERAJU WORKS is designed around a shared structured reference: Budget → Work → Progress → Claim → Payment → VO.
