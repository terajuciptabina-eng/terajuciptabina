01 · EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

The investment proposition

TERAJU WORKS is being developed to structure the residential construction workflow from planning and costing through quotation. The product is founder-developed and connected to practical construction experience.

The immediate investment objective is to move from founder-led product development into a more scalable product, engineering and market-validation operation.

02 · PROBLEM

A fragmented workflow

  • • Scope and project information spread across multiple tools.
  • • Quantities, rates and costs can be difficult to structure consistently.
  • • Homeowner and contractor workflows are often disconnected.
  • • Quotation can become a document output instead of a structured project record.
03 · PRODUCT

TERAJU WORKS today

Homeowner Workspace

New-build and renovation planning.

Contractor Workspace

Construction and renovation costing.

Build Planner

Structured residential build planning.

Renovation Planner

Area, quantity and rate based renovation planning.

Next product expansion · IDPlanner

An already-defined interior design planning concept to be translated into a production-ready product module, following the same founder-led approach used to build Build Planner and Renovation Planner.

04 · BUSINESS MODEL

Commercial hypotheses to validate

  1. B2B software: subscription or service model for contractors.
  2. B2C premium: paid planning, estimating or workflow features for homeowners.
  3. Ecosystem: qualified leads, partners, services or transaction layers.
05 · FUNDING

Capital deployment

  • Engineering capacity
  • Product UX and commercialisation
  • Product expansion, including IDPlanner
  • Market validation and acquisition
  • Infrastructure, analytics and security

IDPlanner is planned product development, not a current revenue claim. The founder has already demonstrated a similar concept-to-product workflow through Build Planner and Renovation Planner.

10 · 12-MONTH MILESTONES

What the first 12 months are intended to accomplish

Q1 · 0–3 months

Start sales and marketing immediately; acquire homeowners and contractors, establish sales and analytics, harden the working product and begin IDPlanner engineering.

Q2 · 4–6 months

Continue acquisition, test pricing, onboard pilots and improve onboarding and workflows using market feedback.

Q3–Q4 · 7–12 months

Increase acquisition, develop strategic partnerships, mature IDPlanner, strengthen repeatable sales channels and prepare verified traction data.

Planning targets, not guaranteed outcomes. Numeric KPIs should be set after baseline analytics, hiring scope and acquisition economics are established.
