01 · EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

The investment proposition

TERAJU WORKS starts with a practical problem in construction costing: homeowners and contractors need clearer visibility into how a project cost is structured. The platform is designed to turn basic inputs such as house area or rooms into detailed, SMM-based cost estimates with work items, quantities, rates and amounts.

The next layer is structured construction data. As projects and contractor rate observations are captured, the product is being developed toward state-based rate structures and market signals. The immediate investment objective is to move from founder-led product development into a more scalable product, engineering and market-validation operation.

02 · PROBLEM

A fragmented workflow

  • • Scope and project information spread across multiple tools.
  • • Quantities, rates and costs can be difficult to structure consistently.
  • • Homeowner and contractor workflows are often disconnected.
  • • Quotation can become a document output instead of a structured project record.
05 · REAL-WORLD PROBLEM SCENARIOS

When budget, work, progress and payment are not connected.

Two real construction scenarios illustrate the underlying problem TERAJU WORKS is designed to address. These are problem scenarios, not claims about TERAJU WORKS customers.

Case 01 · Cluster House · Taman Desiran Bayu

RM250,000 lump-sum contract. Claims were made by percentage without a clear item-level basis; the homeowner had paid more than 95% while physical progress was below 70%. VO and scope changes became difficult to reconcile, and the project reached a funding gap before completion.

Case 02 · Bungalow · Penang

Original budget was RM300,000, but RM420,000 had already been spent — RM120,000 above budget (+40%). The project still had unfinished work and outstanding touch-up / finishing items. The contractor could not continue as funds became insufficient, while the homeowner was unwilling to commit further payment after RM420,000 had already been spent.

Common failure point

Budget → Work → Progress → Claim → Payment → VO were not maintained as one shared structured record. The issue is not necessarily that either party intends to mislead the other; without a common reference, the homeowner can feel overpaid while the contractor can feel underfunded.

03 · COST TRANSPARENCY

Not just “How much?” — but “Where does the money go?”

The core workflow is designed to start with basic project inputs and generate a detailed construction cost estimate structured around SMM rather than relying only on a single lump-sum or RM/sqft figure.

Basic input

House area / rooms and project information.

Detailed estimate

Work items, quantities and rates structured around SMM.

Cost structure

Quantity × Rate × Amount for clearer visibility.

This is a product objective, not a measured claim about reduced discrepancies.
