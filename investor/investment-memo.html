01 · EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

The investment proposition

TERAJU WORKS is being developed to structure the residential construction workflow from planning and costing through quotation. The product is founder-developed and connected to practical construction experience.

The immediate investment objective is to move from founder-led product development into a more scalable product, engineering and market-validation operation.

02 · PROBLEM

A fragmented workflow

  • • Scope and project information spread across multiple tools.
  • • Quantities, rates and costs can be difficult to structure consistently.
  • • Homeowner and contractor workflows are often disconnected.
  • • Quotation can become a document output instead of a structured project record.
